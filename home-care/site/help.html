Your Homecare guide.

Find products. Compare the facts. Review white-label recommendations.

  1. 1. Find products

    Products → Browse competitors opens the U.S. catalog. Filter by retailer, category and brand. Global retailers & items includes country filters and China.

  2. 2. Compare your shortlist

    Select products, then open Products → Compare selected. Review the dated price, pack size, photo and source. Export your selected CSV for Excel.

  3. 3. Review recommendations

    White-label recommendations keeps Homecare’s reference products and proposed improvements. Read the source evidence and validation questions before adopting an idea.

Stay up to date

Open the daily updates section in the product catalog. Checked dates, catalog additions and product launch dates have different meanings. Prices are observations and retain their original dates and currencies.
