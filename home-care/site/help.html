Start here. Follow three simple steps.

Use Products to research the market, then move to the Workshop to develop an idea.

  1. 1. Find products

    Open Products → Browse competitors for the U.S. catalog. Choose a category and retailer, then search for an item. For other countries, choose Products → Global retailers & items. China has its own shortcut.

    Browse U.S. products → · Browse China →

  2. 2. Compare what exists

    Use Select or Select to compare on product cards, then Products → Compare selected. Export selected CSV saves your shortlist for Excel; photos appear as links. In Workshop, the first step shows real national-brand photos, saved observations and source links.

    View national-brand comparisons →

  3. 3. Develop a white-label concept

    Choose a home-care subcategory and concept. Follow Real products → Why & what changes → Design & costs. Review the proposed changes, inspect the 3D model, choose a finish and check the dimensions, BOM and pricing assumptions. Save the configured brief or download the Blender pack.

    Open Workshop →

Music, button sounds and saved work

On product and Workshop pages, open Sound to play ambient music, adjust the volume or turn button sounds off. Product selections stay in this browser. Download your brief and CSV to keep a copy of your work.

How to read prices and proposals

Retail prices are dated source observations. Costs, dimensions and performance targets for concepts require validation. An empty search result is a research gap and does not establish a retailer or Target assortment gap.
