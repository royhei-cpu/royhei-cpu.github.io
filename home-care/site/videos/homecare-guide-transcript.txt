Homecare tutorial — Updated 24 November 2026

Use the top menu to move around Homecare.

Open Products, then Browse competitors. Choose a retailer, type a product name, and use Category to narrow the results. Click Details on a product to see more.

Click Select on the products you want. Open Products, then Compare selected. Click Export selected CSV to download your selection.

Click News and updates in the top menu to open the news page.

Open Insights, then Target inspiration to browse recommendations. Use the search box and filters to narrow them down.

Click Home to return to the start. Help and guide opens this video.
