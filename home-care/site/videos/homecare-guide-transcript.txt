Homecare tutorial — Updated 24 November 2026

Welcome to Homecare. This updated guide follows the current navigation. Start with Products, use News and updates for dated developments, and find Target inspiration under Insights.

Choose Products, then Browse competitors. Pick a retailer and narrow the catalog by search, category or brand. Open Details to check the exact product, pack and saved source evidence. A catalog addition date is not a retailer listing date or a product launch date.

Select products for your shortlist, then open Compare selected from Products. Review photographs, pack sizes, currencies and dated price observations side by side. Export selected CSV saves the selected records with photo and source links; the spreadsheet does not embed the images.

News and updates now opens its own view. Read each dated update and follow the source links. A newly discovered listing is new to this directory, unless there is separate evidence of a new launch. Rechecking an old page does not make it new news.

Under Insights, choose Target inspiration to review Homecare reference products, proposed improvements and questions to validate. There is no separate White-label recommendations tab, and Brands and factories has been removed. These ideas are research proposals, not approved assortment decisions.

Return to Home whenever you need the starting point. Help and guide keeps this video, captions, a download and the transcript together. You can continue browsing, comparing and exporting at the same Homecare address.
