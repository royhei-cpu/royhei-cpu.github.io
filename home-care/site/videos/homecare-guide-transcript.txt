Homecare — tutorial

Welcome to Homecare. This is your dedicated home-care research website, with its own merchant catalog and white-label recommendations. Here’s how to move from a product search to a useful shortlist.

Start with Browse competitors under Products. Choose the retailer first, then enter a brand or product. Category and brand filters help narrow the results. Here, Walmart and Tide bring the matching listings into view. Use Details to check the saved evidence.

Select the products you want, then choose Compare selected. The table brings their photographs, pack descriptions, price observations and sources together. Check pack sizes before comparing prices. Export selected CSV saves your shortlist with photo and source links.

Homecare also keeps White-label recommendations. Choose a platform or search for a direction, then review the reference products and proposed improvements. These are research recommendations with questions to validate, not confirmed market gaps.

Open News and updates for dated developments and new catalog additions. An added record is not automatically a new product launch. Use Global retailers and items to explore other countries, including China. Your research, comparison and recommendations all stay here in Homecare.
