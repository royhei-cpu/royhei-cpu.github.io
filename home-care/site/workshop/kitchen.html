02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

BLS records substantial household work time but does not isolate sink cleaning. Cross-market brush and refill formats offer test inputs.

Proposed change · Drips

Tool-free tray removal and an open drying gap.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: OXO already has a removable drip tray.

Validate: Hold a fixed water dose and inspect for trapped liquid.

Proposed change · Dispensing

Separate measured pump avoids fluid-filled brush handle.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: OXO dispenses soap through the brush.

Validate: Test dosing consistency, leakage and user preference.

Proposed change · Service

Visible head code, ordinary release and refill shelf plan.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: OXO replacement heads already exist.

Validate: Cycle replacement and validate landed spare cost.

Target assortment check

Existing Target dish brushes, dispensers and sink organizers need equivalent checks.

Decision before production

Stop if the dock traps water or participants prefer their existing setup.
