02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

The reviewed Sterilite exterior is approximately 603 × 406 × 175 mm and already shares a lid across sizes. A similar envelope must earn value through identification and service tests.

Proposed change · Compatibility

A distinct HA footprint with visibly coded lid and body.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Sterilite already shares lids between 32 Qt and 66 Qt boxes.

Validate: Fit every proposed height and measure mis-picks.

Proposed change · Service

Replaceable latch without promising weatherproofing.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: IRIS already offers gasketed latching storage.

Validate: Latch cycles, carry/drop and replacement-force checks.

Proposed change · Visibility

Reversible end label pocket and matte task-label zone.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Clear storage bodies are established.

Validate: Find-item timing in stacked household storage.
