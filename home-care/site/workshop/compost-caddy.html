02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

Stack already supplies a 4 L ventilated caddy and OXO emphasizes easy cleaning. The proposal must show fewer trapped scraps and easier full-bucket handling; a new color alone is insufficient.

Proposed change · Capacity

A compact 4 L format; no capacity advantage over Stack.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: OXO lists 1.75 US gal; Stack lists 4 L.

Validate: Measure usable fill, worktop footprint and emptying.
