02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

The reviewed EasyStore is 249 × 119 × 116 mm and Glam has a removable insert. A larger modular unit needs measured handling and drying gains to justify more countertop space.

Proposed change · Compartments

Separate lift-out wet cup and dry tray with text labels.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: EasyStore and Glam already organize multiple countertop items.

Validate: Timed emptying and reassembly with real toiletry items.

Proposed change · Care

Publish tested compatibility boundaries; remove inserts for cleaning.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Umbra warns against alcohol and acetone on its plastic.

Validate: Screen cosmetics, soap and disinfectant labels before any compatibility claim.

Proposed change · Drying

Removable drip tray with a visible emptying cue.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: A non-slip base is already offered by EasyStore.

Validate: Retained-water and dry-time tests under matched humidity.

Target assortment check

Decision before production

Stop if insert access, tip stability or cosmetic-contact resistance is worse than the purchased benchmark.
