02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

MUJI lists a replacement pole button; IKEA lists bristle and textile refills. These support a serviceability concept.

Proposed change · Replacement

A tool-level spare bill with visible part codes and separately purchasable wear parts.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: National-brand systems already sell replacement elements.

Validate: 80% unaided spare identification and successful fitting target.

Proposed change · Access

A distinct serviceable HA-01 connector secured with ordinary screws.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: No cross-brand interface license or fit has been established.

Validate: Tolerance stack, retention and intellectual-property review.

Proposed change · Supply

Propose a five-year spare availability goal and a shipping-cost ceiling.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: No supplier service commitment has been obtained.

Validate: Obtain supplier terms and model delivery cost.

Target assortment check

Existing Target replacement heads remain benchmarks; catalog absence does not prove a gap.

Decision before production

Stop if spare availability or shipping cost makes repair impractical.
