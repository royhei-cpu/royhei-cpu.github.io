02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

Latex and latex-free household gloves are both established. Composition transparency, sizing and task identification require tests, without assuming protective certification.

Proposed change · Material

Nitrile household glove candidate with clear composition labeling.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Marigold discloses natural latex; Vileda offers a latex-free nitrile option.

Validate: Supplier composition, pinhole and household-chemical assessment.

Proposed change · Task identity

Raised task tab and large size marks with non-color coding.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Textured grip is already offered by Marigold.

Validate: Wet handling and task-assignment errors.

Proposed change · Drying

Two reinforced hanging tabs outside the contact area.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: No verified life or drying advantage.

Validate: Cuff tear, donning and dry-time trials.
