02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

MUJI and IKEA demonstrate modular systems. The open question is whether a Target version can reduce stored volume and make replacements simpler.

Proposed change · Compatibility

HA-01 visible connector code and a large push release; no claimed compatibility with other brands.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Shared tools already exist in Joseph Joseph CleanTech.

Validate: Cycle connector 5,000 times and measure release force.

Proposed change · Storage

A draining rail stores three heads separately from the pole.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: No like-for-like measured storage-volume baseline yet.

Validate: Measure the complete stored kit against a purchased comparator.

Proposed change · Service

Publish part IDs, repair steps and replenishment availability for every wear part.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: O-Cedar already offers replacement pads.

Validate: Validate replacement supply and a timed identification task.

Target assortment check

A full review of Target modular tools and Target Plus equivalents is still required.

Decision before production

Stop if the connector fails the durability protocol or spare heads cannot meet cost targets.
