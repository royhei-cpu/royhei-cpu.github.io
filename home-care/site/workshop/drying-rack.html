02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

Two reviewed products already provide 18–25 m drying length. A smaller rack needs a measurable handling, service or storage benefit rather than a claim that folding is new.

Proposed change · Capacity

A smaller 12 m target format with clear load zones.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: HangOn lists 25 m and Pegasus lists 18 m drying length.

Validate: Measure usable rail length, floor area and full-load clearance.

Proposed change · Repair

Screw-retained replaceable rod clips with visible codes.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Replaceability of comparator rail joints not established.

Validate: Replace a damaged clip in under five minutes in a supervised prototype trial.

Proposed change · Safety

Contrasting lock position and guarded pivot covers.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Folding frames already exist.

Validate: Tip, pinch, locking and wet-load checks before release.

Target assortment check

No validated Target assortment gap. Audit current Target national-brand and owned-brand SKUs, pack sizes, prices and availability in this category before an opportunity decision.

Decision before production

Stop for instability, exposed pinch points, coating corrosion or an uneconomic service-part system.
