02 · Make the development case

Why consider white label?

IKEA offers differentiated cloth colors and replacement pads. The hypothesis is better household organization, not a new cloth category.

Proposed change · Textile

Three 300 × 300 mm cloths with stitched task symbols.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Scotch-Brite specifies a 290 × 310 mm microfiber cloth.

Validate: Absorbency, lint, dye transfer and 50-wash pilot checks.

Proposed change · Storage

A removable drip tray and separated drying rails.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: A cloth by itself is already established.

Validate: Measure drying under the same controlled humidity.

Proposed change · Accessibility

Kitchen, bath and general-care text with raised/stitched shapes.

Brand reference & proof needed

Existing products: Color alone may not identify room assignment.

Validate: Measure assignment errors after two weeks.

Target assortment check

Target cloths and sponge cloths already exist; only a validated system difference is relevant.

Decision before production

Stop if users ignore the coding or absorbency falls below the comparator after washing.
