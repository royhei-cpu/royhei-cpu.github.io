## Evidence and limitations

ASINSIGHT pages were discovery leads only. Each accepted ASIN was verified against its Amazon product page. No third-party estimated sales or ranks were imported. Exact source URLs, checked dates, and original image URLs are stored per record. Badges retain the exact rounded Amazon wording. Date First Available is recorded only when explicitly present; it is not a launch date. Regular one-time checkout prices could not be established and remain blank, rather than substituting subscription or other-seller prices.

Amazon search pages returned service errors. The bulk research provider returned an account credit limit. Individual public Amazon pages were accessible. BJs public categories were accessible but could not be exhaustively enumerated; H-E-B direct category access returned HTTP 403. The concurrent audit also added BJs, H-E-B and Dollar Tree product-page evidence; these updates are preserved. Remaining retailers were checked for catalog/UI coverage, not exhaustively re-researched.
