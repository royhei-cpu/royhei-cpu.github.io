What “all” means here: every name in the captured catalog is included. This is not yet a census of every brand or handmade studio in China. New and unverified names remain visible rather than being presented as verified brands.

Reading the labels: “Brand / operator profile” has a linked company or industry identity source. “Industry-listed” is a trade-show discovery lead. “Squishy maker label” comes from a seller and may need identity and origin checks. “Other catalog label” may be a character, series or collaboration. “Supplier lead” is an exhibitor company, not necessarily a manufacturer or Chinese-owned brand.

Product totals count saved listing records / series, not unique designs, live stock, sales or all products offered by a brand. Separate aliases have separate product links; no speculative maker assignment is applied to products.
