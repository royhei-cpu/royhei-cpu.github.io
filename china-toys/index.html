Know what you’re comparing.

This directory brings product evidence together without turning missing data into invented facts.

Products and waves
One card normally represents a product listing or blind-box series—not each possible figure inside. Wave numbers appear only when the source states them. Some cross-retailer naming differences may remain.
Mainland China vs. international
A Chinese brand’s overseas product page is not proof of mainland availability. Currency, retailer and market stay with every price. No currency conversion is presented as a China retail price.
Three different dates
Launch: explicit release evidence, with the market identified. Retailer listed: the storefront’s publication timestamp, which can change after a relisting. Directory added: discovery date. A restock is not a new launch.
Prices
Displayed asking prices are not completed transaction prices. Single boxes, sets and confirmed-character variants are labeled. A documented launch price may differ from today’s retail price.
Units sold
Only source-reported product sales are shown, with channel, period and evidence. Reviews, views, inventory and brand revenue are not unit sales. Not disclosed does not mean zero.
New this week
Newly found products, documented launches, price changes and restocks are separate signals. The initial catalog is a baseline—not a claim that all these products just launched.
Photos and scope
Real product photographs are cached from source listings. Products without a usable photograph stay out of the visual directory. Coverage is expanding and is not complete across every Chinese seller or platform.
