How to use this catalog

Choose a shop or brand, then tap a product for its details and source links.

Products and waves
One card normally represents a product listing or blind-box series—not each possible figure inside. A wave is a numbered release in a series, shown only when the source states it. Different sizes, packaging or variants may have separate cards.
China vs. overseas
A Chinese brand’s overseas page does not prove the item is sold in mainland China. Every price keeps its original currency, shop and country. An overseas price is not presented as a China price.
Three different dates
Launch: when the product was released, according to the linked source. Shop listed: when the shop posted the page. Added to catalog: when the item joined this website. An item returning to stock is not a new release.
Prices
These are advertised prices, not confirmed sale prices. Single boxes, sets and specific characters are labeled. An original launch price may differ from today’s price.
Units sold
A sales count is shown only when a source reports it for that product. The shop and time period are included where available. Reviews, views, stock quantities and brand revenue are not units sold. “Not disclosed” means the number is unavailable—not zero.
This week’s updates
Added to catalog: newly added to this website. New releases: release dates fall in the week shown. Price changes: a shop changed its advertised price. Back in stock: available again at a tracked shop. Items included in the first catalog are not counted as new additions.
Photos and coverage
Photos come from the linked product pages. Items without a usable photo are kept out of the visual catalog. Not every Chinese seller or product is included, and some information is still missing.
