What “checked” means here

Saved URL identities were reconciled against public product feeds, store category pages and selected exact product pages. This is not a manual inspection of all 4,230 product photographs, an authenticity certification, or proof of full mainland assortment. A readable listing is not proof of stock or a completed sale.

The comparison baseline is the catalog’s 29 October full collection and 30 October detail review. This dated report remains a snapshot even when later weekly refreshes change the live catalog.

Findings that change how to use the catalog

Assortment gaps are real

TOYZEROPLUS has 41 direct-store blind-box candidates at its corrected address. Rolife’s figure collection shows 28 items and Molynk’s product pages work, despite zero captured direct-store offers. These are missing source coverage, not evidence of empty stores.

Some saved offers are stale

The audit found 38 price differences and 52 stock differences across 83 variant records belonging to 48 products; seven variants changed in both ways. The public catalog applies these exact-offer corrections and marks the 15 broken links as historical with unknown current availability.

Popularity needs several views

Use reported IP revenue to compare commercial scale within one company; platform chart appearances for repeated channel visibility; and dated product evidence for specific-series demand. Retailer curation and social views are weaker, different signals.

03 / PRODUCT DEMAND AND EMERGING THEMES

What is trending, and how strong is the evidence?

Q3 product demand: a threshold group, not a 1–7 ranking

三文娱’s 13-week Tmall/Douyin review identifies seven series whose sampled pages displayed sales counters above 100,000: SpongeBob Residents of Bikini Bottom, CRYBABY Vacation Mode On, Twinkle Twinkle 怦然星动, YOYO 萌趣歪头, Twinkle Twinkle Sweet Dreams Forecast, DIMOO 新生日记 and DIMOO Secret Theater.

These are page-displayed totals observed in Q3, not verified incremental Q3 units. Limited auctions and releases absent from charts can be missed. The same study describes lower-threshold demand for Heyone’s 心动狂欢4.0 / 星梦奇缘2.0 and TNT SPACE’s Rayan 无垠跳动, DORA 甜酷警告 and Zoraa 糖果过家家mini. Exact series translation and SKU matching are needed before transferring a signal to a catalog card.

三文娱 on Sina · 8 October 2026 ↗. Its 36Kr republication is the same study, not a second independent source.

Analyst implication: Maintain established-series coverage and restock tracking alongside new releases. A new product page alone is insufficient evidence of popularity.

Retailer picks: discovery signals

TOP TOY’s Best Sellers page describes a handpicked collection and opens with featured sorting. KikaGoods Hot Sale mixes toy products with accessories. JOTOYS also uses a Best Sellers label. These observations support retailer merchandising signals; they do not establish numerical nationwide ranks.

Wax-shell squishies: emerging attention

Local reporting cites 340 million Xiaohongshu topic views as of 22 October and interviews sellers of crackling wax-shell squishies. One shop quoted CNY 10–80 retail; another offered a 12-piece blind set for CNY 88. These are observations, not national average prices or buyer counts.

Analyst implication: Evaluate sound-plus-touch appeal alongside breakage, temperature sensitivity and repeat use. Social reach alone cannot establish profitable demand.

Maintenance rules

Weekly collection uses the corrected TOYZEROPLUS address. The dated correction step preserves newer observations and never turns a failed link check into a fresh successful price check. Future source checks should retain HTTP status, final URL, exact variant ID, currency, pack size and evidence period. This report’s market statistics do not automatically become “this week’s sales” during a refresh.
