Start here
Welcome to China Toy Atlas. This tour shows you how to find blind boxes and squishy toys, read the details we have, and make a useful shortlist. You are looking at the actual catalog. It is a growing research directory, not a complete list of every product sold in China.

Weekly updates
Start with this week's updates. Added to catalog means newly added here, not necessarily newly launched. New releases have release dates in the week shown. Price changes and back in stock refer to tracked shops. Click a number to see the matching items, and check the last completed update date.

Choose a shop
Choose a retailer or shop to see its own listings. Here, select Top Toy's international store. The results and weekly counts change with your selection. The price belongs to the shop and market shown. An overseas store price must not be treated as a mainland China retail price.

Search a brand or item
Reset the filters, then choose a brand or type a product name. In this example, search for Crybaby, Cry Me An Ocean. Product cards now put available facts first, such as material, size, character and design options. Missing wave numbers are not repeated as empty labels.

Read all available details
Open Full product details. Read the shop description, available dimensions and materials, and follow their source links. Expand listed options to compare a single blind box with a full set or a specific character. Prices for different packs are not directly comparable. Source photographs may show example designs, not the guaranteed contents of one random box.

Understand the dates
The information still to verify is kept separately. Missing facts are never guessed. A launch date is a release date supported by a source. A shop page date is when that retailer published its listing. They are different. Launches and waves shows supported dates and clearly marked estimates, with the market identified.

Read the trend reason
Open Trending and read the reason on the card, not just the badge. A reported purchase counter, a dated demand report and a retailer's hot pick are different kinds of evidence. Possible product appeal is shown separately as interpretation. A shop recommendation does not prove nationwide popularity or measured sales growth.

Save and export
Use the bookmark on a product photo to save it. Open Shortlist to review your saved items on this device. Download C S V exports the filtered list, including available specifications, source links and price options. The Full C S V link at the bottom downloads the whole catalog. You can open these files in Excel.

Check coverage and share
Finally, open Brand coverage and item checks to see what is partial or missing. Weekly updates use the same website link. Check the actual completed-update date rather than assuming every shop was refreshed. Share this as a product-discovery tool, and verify the original listing before making a purchasing decision.
