China Toy Atlas — tutorial

Welcome to China Toy Atlas. This is your dedicated home for blind boxes, squishies and character toys, with the original character imagery, collections and sidebar.

Start by searching a character, brand or series. Here, Crybaby brings matching products into view. Use the brand, shop, character and product-type filters to narrow the directory. Reset all filters brings the full catalog back.

Choose Select for comparison on the products you want. Open Compare in the sidebar, then Open side-by-side comparison. You can compare two to six products or shop offers. Keep the seller, pack option, market and price date in view.

The comparison includes photographs, source links and available product details. Download comparison CSV saves the evidence and photo links. Download photo sheet gives you a visual reference. A series image does not guarantee the design inside a random blind box.

Open Research and news for Stories and news, or Daily updates. Each update keeps its sources and dates. Catalog additions, announcements and launches are different events. Save products to build your own collection, and come back to Explore whenever you want to discover something new.
