Weekly headline dashboard
Start at the weekly headline dashboard. The dates show the saved reporting period. Catalog additions are newly captured here, not necessarily newly launched products. Click a number to see its matching products. Open Weekly highlights for examples and source links. Current trend evidence is separate from this week’s changes. The dashboard refreshes from the saved catalog each week.

Choose a shop and check products
Choose a retailer to browse its own assortment. Then select a brand, or search for a product or character. Selecting another shop starts a fresh browse and clears conflicting filters. Each card shows the shop, currency, pack and available details. A price from an international store is not a mainland China price. Use Full product details to investigate further.

Read details and original sources
Inside product details, check the description, material, size and available design options. Expand the shop’s listed options to distinguish one random box, a full set and a named character. Check the observation date, then follow the original shop link for current availability. A series photo does not guarantee every design pictured. Missing facts stay out of the main details; source warnings remain visible.

Select products and compare
Use Select for comparison on two to six product cards. You can keep selecting across brands or shops. Then press Compare. The table puts the photos and available details side by side. Use each product’s option menu to choose the exact pack. Compare the same currency and pack basis, and check source dates. There is no automatic best-value claim.

Export CSV and a photo sheet
Download comparison C S V exports only your selected offers, with photo links, descriptions, specifications, prices, currencies, variants and source information. A C S V is a text file, so it cannot embed photographs. Open its Image U R L to view the photo. Download photo sheet gives you a visual comparison with photos and details; it needs internet to load the linked images. You can open it in a browser and print or save it as a P D F.

Export filtered products and verify trends
Close the comparison and use Download C S V in the header to export the current filtered results. Full C S V in the footer exports the entire catalog, including photo links and available details.
