Start here
Welcome to China Toy Atlas. Find Chinese blind boxes, squishies and character toys. Start with a brand, shop or character. Tap a product photo for details. This guide is still growing. Its product counts describe what we have collected, not everything a retailer sells.

Browse a retailer in one tap
Open Retail chains and tap Top Toy. Its captured shop catalog opens immediately. There is no second product link to choose. These are international store listings, with that market's prices. Other retailers may have only a partial listing. A small count means our research is incomplete, not that the shop sells only a few toys.

Find your character
Use Brand, Product type and the search box to narrow your choices. Here we select Pop Mart and search for Cry Me An Ocean. Reset filters returns to browsing. Use the Brands page to explore more makers. A character name and the company that makes it are different things.

Read a product
Tap Full product details. Check the character, size, material and the pictured design. Open the shop options to see whether an offer is one random box, a full set, or a confirmed character. Keep the shop, country and currency in mind. A launch price can be historical. Follow the shop link for today's price and availability.

Compare your picks
Select two to six products, then open Compare. The table places their photographs, prices, packs and available facts side by side. Compare the same pack and currency. Different character options can have different prices. Blank information means unconfirmed, not zero.

Save and download
The bookmark saves favourites on this browser. Open Saved favourites to see them again. Download CSV exports the current filtered list. In Compare, Download comparison CSV saves your selected products, and Download photo sheet gives you an illustrated file. CSV contains photo links, not embedded images. You can open CSV in Excel.

Understand this week and trends
This week shows the saved catalog period and latest source update. Expand it for changes and sources. Added to this guide does not necessarily mean a new product launch. Trending can mean reported demand or a shop's recommendation. Open the evidence to check the date and market. These signals do not automatically establish nationwide rankings or weekly sales growth.

Music and button sounds
Music and click sounds begin after your first tap, when your browser allows audio. Choose Soft, Medium or Lively. Use Sound on to mute, and tap Sound off to enable it again. Your preference is remembered. Brands and Research also have these controls. Music pauses during the tutorial so you can hear the narration.

What a partial listing means
Some shops have many captured products, while others have a small researched sample. Partial listing marks this difference. China release highlights are separate from an international shop catalog. The coverage notes explain remaining gaps. Check the original seller before buying, and use the dates shown to judge how recent each price or release is.
