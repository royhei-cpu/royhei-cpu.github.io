Luggage Atlas — tutorial

Welcome to Luggage Atlas. Your worldwide luggage directory keeps its own travel artwork, brand photography and editorial design. Use it to explore products, compare exact offers and follow the latest dated updates.

Choose Start browsing to open the product directory. Search a brand or model, then refine the product type or other filters. Each card keeps its size, color, market and price observation. Photo labels tell you whether the image matches the exact offer or is a collection reference.

Select Compare this offer on up to four products. Open Compare to see your shortlist side by side. Prices keep their original currencies and dates. Review the product, dimensions, source details and photo evidence before deciding which offers are comparable.

Export selected CSV saves the comparison with source and photo links. You can also export a comparison with photographs. Clear the shortlist whenever you want to begin a new comparison.

News and updates brings together dated launches, promotions and research additions. Open the source to check the original evidence. A catalog addition is different from a product launch. Return to Find luggage for feature filters, or explore the original product gallery for outside and inside views.
