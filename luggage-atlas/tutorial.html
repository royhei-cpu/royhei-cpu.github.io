Read the walkthrough

Start with products. Start with Browse products on the home page.

Find a brand or model. Search a brand or model, then narrow the results by product type.

Narrow your search. Open More filters for region, currency, and photo coverage.

Choose up to four. Check the size, colour, photographs, and dated price on each product. Tick Compare this offer to save your choices. You can select up to four.

Open your comparison. Open Products, then Compare products.

Review and export. Review your shortlist side by side, and export a CSV or a comparison with photos.

Follow news and updates. For launches and catalogue changes, open News and updates.

Explore the research. For research, open Insights. Sales and analysis shows saved sales signals and prices. Target inspiration shows product directions, proposed concepts, and supporting competitor products.

Return home anytime. Use Home to return to the starting page, and Help and guide to replay this walkthrough.
