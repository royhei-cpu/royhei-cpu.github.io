Current workflow · updated 4 November

1. Find the luggage

Choose a brand, region or price range. Tick several features to require all of them. Open a model for photos, dimensions and source evidence.

Try the worldwide finder →

2. Develop Open Story

Opportunities, white space and the workshop now share one page. Use “Use these ideas in Open Story” on a model, or choose individual feature modules. Brief-only options are clearly labeled.

Add features →

3. Inspect and cost

Choose color and finish. Click Inside to open the case, or Exploded to inspect assemblies. Enter quantities and component quotes in the BOM; add custom requirements for anything missing. Export CSV, JSON or your customized GLB.

Build the BOM →
Why are some specifications blank?

Unknown is not absent. The Atlas contains 173 brand profiles, but it does not claim every global model or every feature is verified. Sources and dates stay with each claim. Use custom requirements when your design idea is not yet in the feature library.
