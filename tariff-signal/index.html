Upcoming — tariff-rate quota allocation

USTR allocated the remaining FY2027 WTO raw-cane-sugar tariff-rate quota.

Before: 55,993 metric tons raw value of the FY2027 in-quota quantity had not been assigned by country. After: USTR assigned that remaining quantity among listed eligible countries, allowing covered raw cane sugar to use the lower in-quota tariff treatment within each allocation.

HTS / product scope
Raw cane sugar within the FY2027 WTO TRQ; the official country allocation table and applicable HTS quota provisions control eligibility.
Effective
Eligible in-quota entries may begin Oct 1, 2026 and run through Sep 30, 2027, subject to quota availability.
Legal authority
WTO tariff-rate quota administration · USTR allocation announced Sep 23, 2026
Rules / exclusions
Certificates of quota eligibility are required for allocated countries; allocations to net sugar importers are conditioned on origin verification. Above-quota imports remain subject to the higher tariff treatment.
Confidence
Verified · official USTR announcement
Sourcing takeaway
For raw-cane-sugar orders, confirm the exporting country’s allocation, certificate, and available quota balance before entry; do not treat the allocation as a universal duty reduction.

Sep 10, 2026
Upcoming — import bans and scope changes

Five Section 338 proclamations tighten and recalibrate treatment of specified Canadian products.

Before: specified Canadian alcoholic beverages, dairy products, motor vehicles, and parts were covered by additional duties of up to 50% under the July 20 actions. After: annex-listed products move from the additional duty to an import ban on September 29, while separate annexes add products including certain ATVs and dairy goods and remove products including rock salt and cement from the tariff scope on September 15.

HTS / product scope
Canada-origin products identified in the annexes to five Presidential Proclamations dated Sep 8, 2026: specified alcoholic beverages, dairy products, motor vehicles and parts, plus added and removed tariff lines; the annex language and HTS implementation control.
Effective
Product additions and removals: 12:01 a.m. ET Sep 15, 2026. Import bans: goods imported on or after 12:01 a.m. ET Sep 29, 2026.
Legal authority
Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 and Trade Act §604 · five Presidential Proclamations signed Sep 8, 2026
Rules / exclusions
The Section 338 treatment applies even when goods qualify as originating under USMCA and stacks with Section 232 tariffs. Goods imported before the ban date but not yet entered remain subject to the prior 50% duty where specified. If a ban is invalidated, the proclamation provides for the prior 50% duty to apply to the affected import.
Confidence
Verified · official White House proclamations and USTR statement
Sourcing takeaway
Pause or reroute affected Canada-origin orders scheduled to import on or after Sep 29. Before every Sep 15 shipment, screen the exact HTS line against the five annexes; do not assume USMCA qualification removes this Section 338 exposure.

Sep 3, 2026
Implemented — increased

Section 232 duties on specified unmanned aircraft systems and components took effect.

Before: the products in Annexes I and II were not subject to this new UAS-specific Section 232 duty. After: covered heavy UAS, thermal-imager UAS, docking stations, and listed critical components are subject to 100%; covered UAS of 25 kg or less are subject to 25%.

HTS / product scope
UAS and components identified in Presidential Proclamation Annexes I and II; classification and technical specifications control coverage
Effective
12:01 a.m. ET on Sep 3, 2026; certain Annex III components begin Feb 9, 2027
Legal authority
Section 232 of the Trade Expansion Act of 1962 and Trade Act §604 · Presidential Proclamation dated Aug 13, 2026
Rules / exclusions
Duties generally stack with other charges. Certified products from Japan, Korea, Taiwan, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, or the EU are capped at 15% including Column 1; UK products at 10%. Approved onshoring and specified Blue UAS/FCC products receive separate treatment. Manufacturing drawback is limited.
Confidence
Verified · official presidential proclamation
Sourcing takeaway
Immediately screen all drone, docking-station, thermal-imaging, and critical-component purchases against the annexes; confirm origin certification and onshoring-list eligibility before entry.
